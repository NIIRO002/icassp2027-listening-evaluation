# Blinded analysis plan — temporal-application addendum v1

This plan applies only to `icassp2027_temporal_support_addendum_v1`. It must not be pooled with root-study responses. The private, access-controlled protocol fixes which physical method is the preregistered method for each axis; the public page deliberately contains no decoding key.

## Preregistered hypotheses

- H1 — Intensity controllability: listeners prefer the preregistered temporal-application method for conveying requested vocal intensity.
- H2 — Breathiness localization: listeners prefer the preregistered temporal-application method for localizing breathiness without unnecessary temporal spread.
- H3 — Vibrato localization: listeners prefer the preregistered temporal-application method for appropriate localization to long notes.

The full scientific definitions and direction of H1–H3 are frozen in the local unblinded protocol before participant responses are collected. The public A/B position is balanced and carries no condition meaning.

## Primary analysis

The primary unit is the participant. For each axis, report:

- preregistered-method preference proportion;
- comparison-method preference proportion;
- no-clear-difference proportion;
- 95% bootstrap confidence intervals across participants using 10,000 resamples.

No-clear-difference responses remain a separate category and are not coded as half a vote. A supplementary decisive-response analysis may compare the two methods after removing no-clear-difference responses, but the no-clear-difference rate must still be reported. This rule is fixed before responses are inspected.

## Validity and exclusion rules

Exclude only:

- incomplete studies;
- a trial without both required playback-completion flags;
- a duplicate participant ID when it is a repeated submission rather than an intentional replacement;
- an obvious documented technical failure.

Do not exclude a participant because their response disagrees with a hypothesis. Do not exclude trials post hoc based on outcomes.

## Interpretation policy

- If the preregistered method is clearly preferred and its confidence interval supports the intended direction, the result may support the corresponding temporal-application claim.
- If results are near chance or the confidence interval includes no clear advantage, call the result inconclusive, retain objective temporal-localization evidence, and do not claim perceptual superiority.
- If the comparison method is clearly preferred, report it internally, do not claim perceptual superiority for the preregistered method, and do not manipulate or exclude trials to reverse the result.

This addendum cannot support a claim that temporal application reduces absolute cross-axis interference. The relevant prior experiment did not establish that result.
